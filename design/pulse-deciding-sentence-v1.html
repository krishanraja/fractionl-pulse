Methodology at a glance

One release, three measured dimensions

Source observations are collected daily and interpreted as a weekly market read. The public FWI is a directional composite, not a forecast.

50% Hiring activity

Advertised demand across the six tracked fractional C-suite roles, with financing context treated as context rather than proof of future hiring.

20% Talent availability

Signals describing available fractional leadership supply. Pulse does not publish a per-role supply score.

30% Market buzz

Observed attention and market activity. Attention can move before contracts do, so this component remains contextual.

The six role readings on this page measure advertised demand in the current source window. They do not establish local demand, rates, candidate quality or future movement.

What this release can tell you

  • The current aggregate FWI state
  • How six role-demand readings compare in this release
  • Which inputs are healthy and how complete the release is

What it cannot decide for you

  • Whether one candidate fits one company
  • Your local demand, conversion rate or pricing power
  • What the market will do next
