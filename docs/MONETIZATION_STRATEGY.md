# Pulse Monetization Strategy

**Canonical strategy and evidence:** [CORPORATE_STRATEGY.md](CORPORATE_STRATEGY.md)
**Status:** Pricing hypotheses for validation, not transaction-validated prices

## Commercial model

Pulse separates the public instrument from the paid benchmark.

1. **Public FWI:** Free score, components, history, role pages, brief, REST API, MCP tools, and methodology.
2. **Founding Benchmark Partner:** A paid 90-day validation engagement for qualified fractional-talent firms that can contribute anonymised engagement data.
3. **Benchmark Membership:** A recurring partner benchmark only after the dataset meets coverage and privacy thresholds.
4. **Enterprise and portfolio:** Custom cohorts and service levels only after deeper coverage exists.

Pulse does not monetise individual fractional executives, generic public-API traffic, advertising companies, publishers, or media citations. Those groups may use the public instrument for free.

## Packaging and price tests

| Offer | Price hypothesis | Release gate |
|---|---:|---|
| Public instrument | £0 forever | Live |
| Founding Benchmark Partner | £1,500 for 90 days | Application and data-sharing review, maximum ten organisations |
| Benchmark Membership | £6,000 per year | Ten active partners, 500 verified trailing-12-month engagements, and safe cohorts |
| Enterprise and portfolio license | From £15,000 per year | 1,500 verified engagements, sufficient partner independence, custom cohort coverage, and service-level capacity |

The pilot fee is credited to the first annual plan. The public API and MCP tools remain free under fair-use controls. Paid delivery may include proprietary cohort outputs, exports, embed rights, custom cohorts, and a contracted service level.

## Primary buyer

The first economic buyer is the founder, CEO, managing director, or COO of a specialist fractional-executive marketplace, placement firm, or collective with at least 50 engagements per year and structured engagement data.

They pay to:

- Compare their pipeline and placements with the market
- Price retainers, rates, and fees using more than their own book
- Find role and geography movement earlier
- Support client and board conversations with defensible evidence
- Allocate executive supply with less guesswork

An individual fractional executive is a valuable free user, but not the primary payer. A private-equity portfolio talent team is a secondary buyer only after the data supports multi-company cohorts.

## Validation economics

Target by 31 October 2026:

- 25 qualified conversations
- 10 signed data-sharing letters of intent
- 5 paid £1,500 pilots
- A credible path to 500 verified records

Stop or redesign the paid offer if fewer than five of 25 qualified firms pay, if bespoke delivery makes the pilot uneconomic, or if ten partners cannot produce 500 usable records.

## Price anchors

- [Staffing Industry Metrics](https://www.staffingindustrymetrics.com/packages.html) lists benchmarking membership at £1,800 or US$1,800 annually.
- [TechServe Alliance](https://techservealliance.org/updated-membership-dues/) lists annual dues from US$1,495 to US$14,495 by revenue tier.
- [Bolster](https://www.bolster.com/pricing) lists executive-talent fees far above the proposed benchmark membership, confirming that repeated executive placements carry material economics.

These anchors make the tests plausible. They do not validate Pulse's prices.

## Revenue recognition rules

- Do not count an unpaid pilot, letter of intent, or verbal commitment as revenue.
- Do not call a plan generally available until its release gate is met.
- Do not sell a public-source export as a proprietary benchmark.
- Do not display a cohort below the approved privacy and independent-partner thresholds.
