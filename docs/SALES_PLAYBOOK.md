Supply is People Data Labs profile counts, LinkedIn proxy, GoFractional listings, supply-intent search. Culture is news, prestige media, podcasts, Reddit, HN. > "Job boards give you raw listings. Pulse is a composite that combines listings with a leading indicator from SEC filings, cultural momentum from three news APIs, and supply-side data from four sources. It's a weather forecast vs. a thermometer."

### When they ask about price

> "Free tier covers the headline score and 30-day trend. Pro is $99/mo (or $79/mo billed annually) and gives you the full sub-index breakdown, all 21 signals, 12 months of history, AI-generated insight cards, and custom alerts when they ship. ### When they ask about data integrity

## 7. Disqualification Criteria

Stop selling if any of these are true. Time is more valuable than the close.

- ❌ Prospect asks for fractional roles below C-suite (we track 6 specific roles only)
- ❌ Prospect needs region beyond US/UK with hard SLA (we have partial EU, no APAC)
- ❌ Prospect needs prediction guarantees (e.g. "will the FWI be above 70 next quarter?")
- ❌ Prospect needs raw data redistribution rights (not in our license)
- ❌ Prospect's use case requires real-time (sub-hour) updates — we're daily ingest, weekly composite
- ❌ Prospect wants peer-reviewed academic citation today — methodology is open, but not yet backtested

For these prospects, point them to the free dashboard and re-engage in 6 months.

## 13. AI Sales Agent Operating Manual

This section is **specifically** for AI sales agents reading this doc as context.

### Before every outreach

- Pull `/fwi-api/current` — capture `score.overall`, `score.label`, `score.delta30d`, `topMovers[0]`
- Pull recipient's last 5 LinkedIn posts → identify trigger signals
- Match to ICP-1 through ICP-7 above
- Select corresponding sequence (A, B, or C)
- Personalize **every** line — name, role, recent post, current FWI score

### Always include

- Current FWI score in the first message
- One specific role-level data point if recipient has a role focus
- A free, no-auth API curl as a CTA
- Founding-customer pricing line if conversation reaches pricing

### Never include

- Made-up customer names or testimonials
- Predictive accuracy figures (e.g. "85% accurate")
- Claims of years of historical data (we have ~12 weeks + accumulating)
- "Live Stripe checkout" claims (still pending)
- Fractional roles below C-suite (only 6 roles tracked)
- Disparagement of named competitors

### Stop conditions

- Prospect explicitly opts out → mark dead, no follow-ups
- Prospect asks not-yet-supported feature (real-time, non-C-suite roles, APAC region) → disqualify, point to free tier, re-engage in 6 months
- Prospect asks for guarantees → don't give them, cite methodology transparency instead
- Three no-responses → stop sequence

### Escalation

If the prospect's deal value would be > $5K/mo or requires custom indices, escalate to a human at `data@fractionl.ai` with the full conversation history attached.
