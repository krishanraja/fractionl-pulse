# Pulse Sales Playbook

**Canonical strategy:** [CORPORATE_STRATEGY.md](CORPORATE_STRATEGY.md)
**Current offer:** Free public instrument plus an application-only Founding Benchmark Partner pilot

## Who to contact

Prioritise US and UK specialist fractional-executive marketplaces, staffing and placement firms, and collectives that:

- Place several C-suite functions
- Complete at least 50 engagements a year
- Have approximately 5 to 50 staff or £1 million to £30 million in revenue
- Store structured engagement data in an ATS, CRM, or marketplace
- Lack an independent market-intelligence function
- Can contribute anonymised records under a data agreement

Economic buyer: founder, CEO, managing director, or COO. Useful champions: head of operations, research, product, or sales.

Do not run a paid sequence against solo executives, single-hire companies, generic staffing firms, adtech, publishers, media, journalists, or AI-agent builders. Let them use and share the public FWI.

## The opening

> You can see your own book, but can you tell whether a slow month is your pipeline or the fractional market? Pulse is building a cross-firm benchmark for fractional leadership. The public index is free. We are selecting ten founding firms that can contribute anonymised engagement data and test a private cohort benchmark.

## Discovery

Ask these before presenting a pilot:

1. How many fractional or interim leadership engagements did you open and fill in the last 12 months?
2. Which roles and geographies account for most of the book?
3. How do you decide whether a pipeline change is firm-specific or market-wide?
4. Which pricing, supply, or expansion decisions rely on your own sample today?
5. What external evidence do clients, boards, or investors ask for?
6. Where are role, geography, opened date, filled date, rate band, duration, and outcomes stored?
7. What privacy or contractual limits would prevent anonymised aggregation?
8. What output would make a £1,500, 90-day pilot worth paying for?

## Qualification

Advance only when the prospect has:

- A repeated decision, not general curiosity
- Enough engagement volume to contribute useful records
- A named buyer and data owner
- A plausible privacy-safe export path
- Willingness to pay for the pilot

Disqualify when the prospect wants individual rate advice, a generic API plan, raw partner records, a one-off media statistic, or exposure in exchange for data.

## Founding Benchmark Partner offer

**Price hypothesis:** £1,500 for 90 days, credited to the first annual plan.

**Capacity:** Maximum ten organisations.
**Status:** Application-only and not generally available.

The pilot includes:

- Data dictionary and privacy review
- Schema and data-quality assessment
- Private cohort prototype, where coverage permits
- One benchmark review with the economic buyer and operating team
- A written record of what the data can and cannot support

It does not include individual records from other partners, guaranteed predictions, personal rate advice, custom consulting outside the agreed benchmark, or a promise that every requested cohort will be publishable.

## Objection handling

### “Go Fractional already publishes benchmarks.”

Correct. Its public demand and compensation pages are useful and free. Pulse should not pretend otherwise. The partner hypothesis is different: compare a firm's own engagement book with a neutral, multi-partner cohort while preserving privacy. If that comparison does not change a repeated decision, the firm should use the public data and not buy the pilot.

### “Why should we share data?”

Because no individual firm's sample can become the market benchmark. The exchange must be reciprocal, privacy-safe, and useful: qualified partners contribute agreed anonymised fields and receive a cohort view that no single participant could create alone. If privacy and independence thresholds cannot be met, the cohort is suppressed.

### “Why pay before the dataset is mature?”

The pilot funds a concrete schema, quality, and cohort test against the firm's own records. It is not a prepayment for a finished annual product. The fee is credited if the annual benchmark launches. If the pilot cannot produce a decision-useful output, that is a validation failure, not a reason to invent more claims.

### “Can we just buy the API?”

The public score, history, brief, role pages, REST API, and MCP tools are free. The paid value is proprietary cohort data, custom delivery, and a service level, not a larger quota for the same public number.

## Product demo order

1. Current public score and role movement
2. Data completeness, source health, and limitations
3. The prospect's repeated decision and current workaround
4. A conceptual private cohort comparison, clearly labelled as a prototype
5. Data fields, privacy rules, release gates, and pilot terms

Do not lead with the number of sources or AI. Lead with the decision the buyer cannot make confidently today.

## CRM stages and proof

| Stage | Required evidence |
|---|---|
| Qualified | Firmographic fit, 50+ annual engagements, repeated decision, named buyer |
| Data-fit | Named data owner, available fields, privacy review path |
| Pilot proposed | Written output, scope, price, and suppression rules |
| Pilot won | Signed agreement and paid invoice |
| Annual candidate | Decision-useful pilot and release threshold achieved |

## Metrics

- Qualified conversation to data-fit rate
- Data-fit to paid-pilot rate
- Paid pilot amount and delivery effort
- Time to first usable record
- Verified records per partner
- Pilot-to-annual conversion after the release gate

No vanity outreach target can compensate for low paid conversion or unusable data.
